# Batch 3 previews — breach, data rights and deletion (STYL-264)

Rendered from the registered templates for Stuart to review. These are sample facts, not a live notice.
Nothing is sent unless `SYSTEM_EMAILS_BATCH3_ENABLED` is exactly `true`. The flag is off by default.
Package 5a adds notices #24–#31 to this file. UK GDPR timing for #36 and #53 is not stated in these emails. Solicitor confirmation is still pending.

## #36 system.batch3.breach-salon

### Subject

Personal data breach notice

### Plain text

```
Hello,

We are writing to Harbour Salon about a personal data breach affecting personal data we process for you. The Terms (clause 6A.7), the Data Processing Addendum (clause 9.1) and Privacy Policy Part F describe this notice.

What happened: a copy of booking names was sent to the wrong internal mailbox and then deleted.

People affected: customers with a booking in March 2027.

Records affected: booking name and phone number.

Likely consequences: those contact details may have been seen by a member of staff who does not work on that account.

Measures taken or proposed: the mailbox copy was deleted and access to that mailbox was removed.

Contact: StyleFlo support.

We became aware on 1 March 2027.

This notice cannot be switched off in your account.

Reply to this email and it will reach StyleFlo support.
```
