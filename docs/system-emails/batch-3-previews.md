# Batch 3 previews — legal notices #24–#31 (STYL-264)

## #24 system.batch3.sub-processor-change

Notice of a sub-processor change

### Plain text

```
Hello,

We are writing to Harbour Salon about a change to a sub-processor. The Terms (clause 6A.3) and the Data Processing Addendum (clauses 6.2 and 6.4) require this notice at least 14 days before the change.

Supplier: Example Supplier Ltd.

What they do: stores conversation transcripts for the assistant.

The change starts on 1 April 2027.

If you object, reply to this email. You may also end the Terms and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

This notice cannot be switched off in your account.

Reply to this email and it will reach StyleFlo support.
```
