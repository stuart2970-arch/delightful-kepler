## Staff 
This is a dual purpose entry, it is used in the following areas
- [[Webpage]]
- [[Google Calendar]]
- It is used to dynamically price products/ services

## Contents
The form to fill in for a Team Member/ Colleague contains the following;
- Staff Member Name - Change to Colleague Name
- Job Title/ Role - allows for different industries (Technician, Stylist)
- eMail Address - [[when the colleague is saved, a calendar with the staff member name will be created if google is authorised]]
- Profile Photo/ Avatar]]
- Google calendar ID - will be populated automatically when calendar is created so can be read only
- Specialist Services and specialities - allows users to shout out any extra services they offer
- Professional Bio - Time, experience, trained under
