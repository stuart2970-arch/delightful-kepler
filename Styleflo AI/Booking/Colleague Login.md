## Colleague Login
When a b2b admin creates a user in the [[Dashboard]], the calendar for that colleague must be auto created, and the email address of the colleague is added

At creation of a staff member, the email address is requested, that email address must be sent a magic login link in a welcome email that remains live for xxx minutes.
When the colleague logs in using the magic link, they must be forced to create a password, or connect a google account to their account
When inside the dashboard the colleague must only have access to the ((staff)) area and their own data (Bio and rota)

In the dashboard the b2b admin must be able to 're-send' the magic link if the colleague has not clicked it before the time runs out
